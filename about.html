About

Waddup World!

I'm a Miami-based poet, musician, and technologist.

I make narrative rap songs about whatever I want to understand—whether it's the perspective of future space travelers or a tea master. (Big ups to Unc Iroh !)

My work can be thought of as research in the form of rap.

I also build hardware and software tools for music-making and education.

Challenging and exploring what's around me,
inviting others to join in the process, and creating interactive experiences is at the heart of what I do.
